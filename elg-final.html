ELG 201 · Module 07

🎯 Can / Can't — Ability & Possibility

Modal verb Can | Vocabulary: Hobbies & Skills

📐 Grammar: Can / Can't

Can A modal verb expressing ability or possibility. Same for ALL subjects — never add "s" in third person.

✅ Affirmative: Subject + can + base verb

SubjectExampleNote
II can speak Spanish.✓
He / She / ItHe can swim well.✓ NOT "cans"
We / TheyThey can play guitar.✓

❌ Negative: cannot / can't

FormExample
cannot (formal)I cannot play football.
can't (informal)She can't speak French.

❓ Questions: Can + Subject + base verb?

QuestionShort Answer
Can you speak Japanese?Yes, I can. / No, I can't.
Can I sit here please?Yes, you can.
What can we do on Saturday?We can go to the park.

📚 Vocabulary: Hobbies & Skills

Hobby / SkillCan sentence
ClimbingI can go climbing on weekends.
PaintingShe can paint beautiful pictures.
CookingHe can cook Italian food.
ReadingThey can read technical manuals easily.
Walking / HikingWe can walk for hours.
ShoppingI can't go shopping today.

📝 Practice (from curriculum)

#Sentence✓ Answer
1Ali can _____ the car. (driving / drives / drive)drive
2They _____ go to the mall — it is raining.can't
3I _____ speak French. (don't can / not can / can't)can't
4Sara can _____ spicy food. (ate / eats / eat)eat
⚠️ Common Errors: NEVER say "He cans swim" or "She don't can drive". Correct: He can swim. She can't drive.

✨ Adjectives — Describing People & Things

Adjective order | Appearance | Personality | Technical

📐 What is an Adjective?

Adjective A word that describes a noun. Comes before a noun (a kind man) or after "to be" (She is smart). Same form for singular and plural.

Adjective Order: Opinion → Size → Age → Color → Noun

OpinionSizeAgeColorNoun
beautifulbignewredcar ✓
nicesmall——bag ✓

👤 Vocabulary: Appearance

New / OldShe wears new clothes.

⏪ Past Simple — Was / Were

was / weren't | Affirmative, Negative, Questions | History vocabulary

📐 Grammar: was / were

SubjectAffirmative ✅Negative ❌
I / He / She / Itwaswasn't (was not)
You / We / Theywereweren't (were not)

Examples

AffirmativeNegative
I was at the park yesterday.I wasn't at school yesterday.
She was happy last night.He wasn't tired.
They were at the museum.They weren't late.

❓ Questions: Was/Were + Subject + …?

QuestionShort Answer
Was she at the library?Yes, she was. / No, she wasn't.
Were they in Jeddah last week?Yes, they were. / No, they weren't.
Where were you yesterday?I was at home.

📚 Vocabulary: Places & History

WordArabicExample

JourneyرحلةThe journey was long and difficult.

📝 Practice

SentenceAnswer

ELG 201 · Module 11

📅 Past Simple — Regular Verbs (+ed)

verb+ed | didn't + base verb | Months of the Year

📐 Grammar: Regular Verbs

Regular Verbs in Past Simple Add -ed to the base verb: work → worked, visit → visited, play → played.
FormStructureExample
✅ AffirmativeSubject + verb+edI worked yesterday. She visited.
❌ NegativeSubject + didn't + base verbI didn't work. He didn't visit.
❓ Yes/No QDid + subject + base verb?Did you study last night?
❓ Wh- QWh- + did + subject + base verb?Where did they travel?
⚠️ In negative and questions, use the BASE verb (NOT +ed): "She didn't go" NOT "didn't went". "Did he prepare?" NOT "did he prepared?"

⚡ Past Simple — Irregular Verbs

10 essential irregular verbs | Workplace context sentences

📐 Grammar: Irregular Verbs

Irregular Verbs Do NOT add -ed. Each verb has its own special past form — must be memorized.
Structure
✅ Subject + Past verb  |  ❌ Subject + did not + base verb  |  ❓ Did + Subject + base verb?

📚 10 Key Irregular Verbs

Base FormPast SimpleWorkplace Example
gowentThe manager went to an important meeting.
seesawThe officer saw a problem during the inspection.
eatateThe staff ate lunch together in the cafeteria.
drinkdrankThe team drank coffee before the conference.
runranThe trainer ran a workshop for new employees.
havehadThe accountant had a meeting about the budget.
taketookThe supervisor took action after the accident.
buyboughtThe company bought new computers.
meetmetThe director met journalists last week.
makemadeThe students made a business plan in class.

📝 Practice

SentenceAnswer
Yesterday I _____ (go) to school.went
She _____ (eat) pizza last night.ate
They _____ (see) a movie on Friday.saw
Omar _____ (run) in the park yesterday.ran
I ____ to the park. (did not went / did not go)did not go
_____ she see her friend? (Did / Do)Did
⚠️ NEVER say "goed", "eated", "runned". In negatives/questions: always BASE form → "She didn't eat" NOT "didn't ate".

Sequencing words · Imperatives · Passive Voice intro

📐 Sequencing Words

WordArabicExample
Firstفي البدايةFirst, install the application.
Next / Thenبعد ذلكNext, activate the system.
After thatبعدهاAfter that, connect the device.
Finallyفي النهايةFinally, submit the form.

💬 Process Vocabulary

CategoryWords
Process Languageprocess, step, instruction, procedure, method
Action Verbsinstall, activate, connect, submit, complete

🔤 Imperatives for Instructions

Imperatives Commands that start with a verb. Used in technical instructions, manuals, and step-by-step guides.
ImperativeExample
OpenOpen the settings menu.
ClickClick the login button.
Go toGo to the dashboard.
SelectSelect your language.
InstallInstall the application first.

🔄 Passive Voice — Introduction

Passive Voice Focus on what IS DONE, not who does it. Common in technical & scientific writing.
Object + is / are + past participle
ActivePassive
The system installs the update.The update is installed automatically.
The cloud stores data.Data is stored in the cloud.
The system detects errors.Errors are detected by the system.

📝 Vocabulary Quiz Practice

QuestionAnswer

What does "Method" mean?A way of doing something
